- Fix, with no advisory yet: SCIM changes, deactivations and deletions from an identity provider now take effect: since 0.1.0 eduLIMS answered a SCIM `PUT`, `PATCH` or `DELETE` with a web page or a `406` instead of applying it, so a person the directory deactivated or deleted kept their eduLIMS sessions and could still sign in. After the upgrade, restart provisioning in the identity provider (a full sync) so it sends every change it could not deliver, and check the provider's provisioning log for failed updates (#365, B4).
