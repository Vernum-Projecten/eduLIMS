- Fix, with no advisory yet: SCIM changes from an identity provider now take effect. Since 0.1.0 eduLIMS answered a SCIM `PUT`, `PATCH` or `DELETE` with a web page or a `406` instead of applying it, so a person the directory deactivated or deleted kept their sessions and could still sign in, and a member removed from a group mapped to `admin` or `teacher` kept that role. After the upgrade, restart provisioning in the identity provider (a full sync), then review the roles of every user shown as "Provisioned by <provider>", administrators and teachers first, against the directory's groups, because the provider may not resend a role change or a deactivation it already counts as sent (#365, B4).
