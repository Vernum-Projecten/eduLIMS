- An uploaded Gen5 plate is checked before any value is used: each of the 96 wells must hold one value within the decimal bounds of the tolerance check, and a team can link a well only to a sample of its own lab, by the sample's lab number. A position outside the plate, a value outside those bounds, a well linked twice, a missing or mistyped lab number and an unknown sample are each refused with the well they concern, all at once.
