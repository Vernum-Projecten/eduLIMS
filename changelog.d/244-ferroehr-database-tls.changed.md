- FerroEHR and the `ferroehr-definitions` one-shot connect to PostgreSQL over TLS and verify the server certificate and host name (`sslmode=verify-full`) against the same CA eduLIMS trusts, `config/postgres-tls/ca.crt` (Q-SEC-1, ADR 0013). Upgrade step: take the new `compose.yaml` from the deploy bundle and make sure `config/postgres-tls/ca.crt` is readable to the FerroEHR container (mode `0644`); a server certificate without `DNS:postgres` in its subject alternative names, or one signed by another CA, now stops FerroEHR from starting.
