- Two changes at the same moment can no longer leave eduLIMS without an administrator who can sign in. `pnpm admin:revoke`, the role edit and the block on the Users screen and the user deletion now wait for each other, a blocked administrator no longer counts, and the later change is refused when it would leave none; the Users screen says so in its own message. An administrator who lost the role while their request ran is refused. A user who loses a role on the Users screen or through `pnpm admin:revoke` is signed out at once. The sign-in library's role, rename and account-creation endpoints (`/api/auth/admin/set-role`, `/api/auth/admin/update-user`, `/api/auth/update-user`, `/api/auth/admin/create-user`, `/api/auth/admin/ban-user`) answer 404, and a call to one of them is in the activity log (B4).
