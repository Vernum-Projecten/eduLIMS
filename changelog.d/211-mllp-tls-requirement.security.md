- The deployment guide now states what FerroBRIDGE 0.0.24 requires for the HL7 v2 listener: MLLP over TLS, the `[hl7v2.tls]` files, and a client certificate with a `certificate_subject` for every `[[hl7v2.senders]]` entry, or FerroBRIDGE does not start. The Afinion 2 has no documented TLS, so keep `[hl7v2] enabled = false` until the route is chosen (a TLS sidecar per analyser or a FerroBRIDGE change); the bind address and the firewall allow-list stay. Check your `ferrobridge.toml` with `docker compose run --rm --no-deps ferrobridge config check`.
