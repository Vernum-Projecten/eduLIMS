- Removing or moving a student whose email address is not verified no longer deletes a waiting address, which may be a classmate's, and adding that address to a lab no longer undoes the student's removal. A student a teacher removed is no longer linked back through a waiting address at the next sign-in; only a teacher adding the address of a verified account again lets them back, and the refused link shows once per removal in the activity log. The removal and move entries in the activity log show how many waiting addresses went (0 or 1).
