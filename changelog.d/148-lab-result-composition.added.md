- A released result of a patient goes to the patient's record in FerroEHR as a Final composition on the `eduLIMS-lab.v1` template, with the lab number, the sample number, the material, the request and the analyser, or the fixed device "eduLIMS manual entry" for a result entered by hand. A retry never writes it twice; when FerroEHR does not answer, the release stands, the results page shows the result as not yet in the patient's record, and eduLIMS tries again every minute. A release waits until the patient's record exists (S15, Q-COMP-1, Q-TRACE-1). Upgrade steps: set `FERROEHR_FACILITY_ISSUER` and `FERROEHR_FACILITY_ID` to the `facilities` entry of eduLIMS's `[[authz.system_client]]` in `config/ferroehr.toml`, and `FERROEHR_TERRITORY` to the ISO 3166-1 code of your country (`.env.example`); the upgrade runs a database migration.
