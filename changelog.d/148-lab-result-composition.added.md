- A released result of a patient goes to the patient's record in FerroEHR as a Final composition on the `eduLIMS-lab.v1` template, with the lab number, the sample number, the material, the request and the analyser; a retry never writes it twice, and when FerroEHR does not answer, the release stands, the result stays pending and eduLIMS tries again every minute (S15, Q-COMP-1, Q-TRACE-1). A release waits until the patient's EHR exists. Upgrade steps: set `FERROEHR_FACILITY_ISSUER` and `FERROEHR_FACILITY_ID` to the `facilities` entry of eduLIMS's `[[authz.system_client]]` in `config/ferroehr.toml`, and `FERROEHR_TERRITORY` to the ISO 3166-1 code of your country (`.env.example`); the upgrade runs a database migration.
